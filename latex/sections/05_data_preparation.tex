\section{Data Preparation}

Raw data was processed in Python (Pandas) through a multi-stage pipeline.
After cleaning, $2\times$IQR outlier removal on \texttt{Duration} and
\texttt{Distance(mi)}, and exclusion of 2023 data (only 3 months of records,
creating temporal skew), the dataset yields \textbf{ 6,162,346 usable
rows across 2016--2022}.

\subsection{Preprocessing Pipeline}
\begin{table}[!htbp]
\centering\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3cm} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{4cm} X @{}}
  \toprule
  \textbf{Stage} & \textbf{Action} & \textbf{Rationale} \\ \midrule

  Column Selection
    & 21 of 46 features retained
    & Focus on accident context, weather, temporal, and spatial signals \\

  Missing Values
    & Median fill for numerics; 'Unknown' for categoricals; drop on critical fields
    & Preserve data volume while removing unreliable records \\

  Datetime Parsing
    & \texttt{Start\_Time} and \texttt{End\_Time} parsed to datetime
    & Enables temporal feature extraction \\

  Year Filter
    & 2023 excluded
    & Only 3 months of 2023 records; partial year creates temporal skew \\

  Outlier Removal
    & $2\times$IQR on \texttt{Duration\_Min} and \texttt{Distance(mi)}; Temperature: $-30$--$130^\circ$F; Lat/Lng within CONUS bounds
    & Remove sensor errors and implausible values (e.g.\ 16-hour road closure, 8.5-mile single-accident road blockage) \\

  Deduplication
    & Unique on accident ID
    & Prevent double-counting from multi-source collection \\

  \bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption{Preprocessing pipeline summary}
\end{table}

\subsection{Weather Grouping Logic}

The raw \texttt{Weather\_Condition} field contains hundreds of distinct string
values. These were mapped to 7 groups using keyword matching:
\begin{table}[!htbp]
\centering\small
\begin{tabular}{@{} l p{9cm} @{}}
  \toprule
  \textbf{Group}  & \textbf{Keywords matched (case-insensitive)} \\ \midrule
  Snow/Ice   & snow, sleet, ice, blizzard, wintry \\
  Fog/Haze   & fog, mist, haze, smoke, smog \\
  Rain       & rain, drizzle, shower, thunder, storm \\
  Cloudy     & cloud, overcast, partly \\
  Clear      & fair, clear, sunny \\
  Wind/Dust  & wind, dust, sand \\
  Other      & anything not matched above \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Keyword-based weather grouping rules}
\end{table}

\subsection{Rationale for Outlier Removal ($2\times$IQR)}

The $2\times$IQR method was chosen over standard $1.5\times$IQR to retain
genuine long-duration incidents while removing sensor errors and timestamp
mistakes. The distribution plots (V\_B1, V\_B2) directly validate this choice:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item Wyoming pre-clean maximum distance: \textasciitilde8.5 miles (physically
        implausible for a single accident).
  \item Maine pre-clean maximum duration: \textasciitilde950 minutes (\textasciitilde16 hours),
        almost certainly a timestamp entry error.
  \item Post-cleaning, the cross-state box shapes are consistent, confirming
        structural soundness of the cleaned dataset.
\end{itemize}
