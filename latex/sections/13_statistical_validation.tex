\section{Statistical Validation}

Statistical tests were conducted to support or challenge the visual patterns
observed in H1 and H2. All tests were run in Python (\texttt{scipy.stats}) on
the cleaned 451,829-row dataset (2016--2022, $2\times$IQR outlier removal).

\begin{quote}
  \itshape
  \textbf{Note on statistical significance at scale:} With $n \approx 450$K,
  even trivially small differences produce significant $p$-values. Effect size
  measures (Cram\'{e}r's $V$, Cohen's $d$) are therefore reported alongside
  $p$-values to assess \emph{practical} significance.
\end{quote}
